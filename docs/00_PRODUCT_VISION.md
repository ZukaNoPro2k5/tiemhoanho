# Product Vision — TiemHoaWeb

## Vision

Create the cutest, most satisfying flower-shop game that can be opened from a link and enjoyed immediately on a phone.

The fantasy is not “maximize revenue.” It is:

> “This is my little flower shop. I make beautiful bouquets for people and slowly make the shop feel like mine.”

## Audience

Primary audience:

- Vietnamese users.
- Primarily women, roughly 16–30.
- Casual/mobile-first behavior.
- Interested in cozy aesthetics, customization, cute characters, small stories and shareable creations.

The product must also remain welcoming outside this demographic; do not encode stereotypes into mechanics or copy.

## Product pillars

### 1. Make something beautiful
Every order ends with a visually appealing bouquet that is uniquely attributable to the player's choices.

### 2. Tiny human stories
Customers have reasons for buying flowers. Requests provide emotional meaning rather than functioning as raw recipes.

### 3. Cozy ownership
Progression should make the flower shop increasingly personal and beautiful, not merely more efficient.

### 4. Light management
Inventory, freshness, money and special days create decisions without turning the game into a spreadsheet.

### 5. Share by desire, not coercion
Players share because the bouquet/card looks good, not because rewards force them to spam friends.

## Product hierarchy

When priorities conflict:

`Delight > clarity > responsiveness > depth > breadth`

## Experience statement

A first-time player should understand how to make and deliver their first bouquet within two minutes without reading a manual.

By the end of the first session, they should have at least one bouquet worth saving to the Flower Diary.
