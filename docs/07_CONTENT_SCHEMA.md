# Content Schema and Seed Content

## 1. Purpose

Game content must be data-driven so writers/designers can add orders without editing React components.

## 2. Initial flowers

Seed with 12 definitions:

1. Hồng đỏ — romantic, warm — red — focal.
2. Hồng hồng — gentle, romantic — pink — focal.
3. Tulip hồng — gentle, romantic — pink — focal.
4. Tulip vàng — cheerful, warm — yellow — focal.
5. Hướng dương — cheerful, supportive, warm — yellow — focal.
6. Cúc họa mi — fresh, gentle, cheerful — white/yellow — secondary.
7. Cẩm chướng hồng — grateful, gentle — pink — secondary.
8. Cẩm tú cầu — elegant, gentle — blue/purple — focal.
9. Baby's breath — gentle, fresh — white — filler.
10. Lavender — gentle, elegant — purple — filler.
11. Eucalyptus — fresh, elegant — green — foliage.
12. Ruscus/leaf stem — fresh — green — foliage.

Exact semantic tags must use the canonical `MoodTag` union from the data model; add tags deliberately rather than ad hoc.

## 3. Materials

Seed:

- 6 wraps: cream paper, blush paper, kraft, sage, translucent white, soft lilac.
- 6 ribbons: cream, dusty pink, burgundy, sage, yellow, lavender.
- 8 cards/stickers: minimal thank-you, heart, birthday, graduation, apology, blank floral, star, smile.

## 4. Customer writing style

Customer lines should feel like actual chat speech, not quest descriptions.

Good:

> “Mai bạn thân mình tốt nghiệp á. Bạn ấy mê màu vàng, mình có tầm 250k thôi.”

Avoid:

> “Objective: Create a yellow graduation bouquet under 250,000 VND.”

## 5. Order template examples

### Graduation / cheerful

- budget: 220k–300k
- tags: cheerful, supportive
- preferred colors: yellow

### First date / gentle

- budget: 180k–260k
- tags: gentle, romantic
- preferred colors: pink, white

### Apology / sincere warm

- budget: 250k–350k
- tags: warm, gentle
- preferred colors: optional

### Thank-you teacher / grateful

- budget: 200k–320k
- tags: grateful, elegant
- preferred colors: optional

### Encourage a friend / cheerful supportive

- budget: 150k–240k
- tags: supportive, cheerful
- preferred colors: yellow, white

## 6. Content safeguards

- Do not imply every romantic gift is heterosexual.
- Avoid humiliating or cruel customer jokes.
- Avoid turning women customers into stereotypes about shopping/relationships.
- Keep drama light and cozy.
- Do not use real personal names/data sourced from players.
